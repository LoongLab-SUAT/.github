## About LoongLab

**LoongLab** is a research group at **Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology (SUAT)**, led by **Dr. Jialong Li**. We study computer networks and distributed systems for AI infrastructure, with a focus on optical interconnects, reconfigurable data center networks, and coordinated compute–network resource allocation.

Our goal is to build high-performance, energy-efficient, and resilient networked systems for emerging compute-intensive applications.

## Research

| Direction | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Data Center Networks** | Routing, transport protocols, and traffic scheduling for reconfigurable data center networks. |
| **Optical Networking** | Optical interconnect architectures for AI clusters and fast-switched optical networks. |
| **Distributed Communication** | Collective communication optimization and coordination across distributed training infrastructure. |
| **Compute–Network Coordination** | Joint allocation of networking, computing, and storage resources; edge computing and AI-enabled network optimization. |

## Principal Investigator
